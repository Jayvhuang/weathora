Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-27

Weathora is a free, browser-based weather & atmospheric toolkit. This policy explains what data we collect and how we handle it.

1. What we do NOT collect

All tools on Weathora run entirely in your web browser using client-side JavaScript. The numbers you enter — temperatures, humidity, wind speed, pressure — are never uploaded to our servers and are not transmitted to us in any form.

2. Cookies and similar technologies

We may use third-party cookies served by Google (including Google AdSense). These cookies help display relevant advertising and may include identifiers used for advertising personalization and measurement. We do not place advertising manually; any ads are shown automatically by Google's Auto Ads.

3. Web beacons

Third-party partners such as Google may use web beacons (also called pixel tags or clear GIFs) to measure advertising impressions and interactions. A web beacon is a tiny invisible image or script that reports when a page is viewed.

4. IP address

When you request pages from our website, your IP address is processed by our hosting provider and by third-party services (such as Google) for security, analytics, and advertising purposes. We do not use your IP address to reconstruct or store the inputs you provide to our tools.

5. Advertising and Google's use of data

Google and its partners may use cookies and web beacons to serve ads based on your prior visits. You can learn how Google uses data when you use our site and manage your choices here: How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps.

6. Amazon Associates

Weathora is a participant in the Amazon Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program. As an Amazon Associate, Weathora earns from qualifying purchases. Some links may be affiliate links.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, you can reach us through the project repository linked from the homepage.

5. 广告与 Google 的数据使用

Google 及其合作伙伴可能会使用 Cookie 和网络信标，根据你的过往访问行为投放广告。你可以在此处了解 Google 如何在使用我们网站时使用数据并进行管理：Google 如何使用数据（合作伙伴网站或应用）。
