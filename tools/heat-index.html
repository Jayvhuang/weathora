Frequently asked questions

Is the heat index calculator free?

Yes. It is completely free, with no account and no limits.

Which formula does it use?

It uses the official NWS Rothfusz regression, the same model used by the U.S. National Weather Service.

Can I enter Celsius?

Yes. Use the unit toggle next to the temperature field to switch between °C and °F.
